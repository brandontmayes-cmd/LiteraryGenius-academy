Privacy Policy

Word Wizards — Literary Genius Academy, LLC

Last Updated: February 9, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Word Wizards! This Privacy Policy explains how Literary Genius Academy, LLC ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you or your child uses the Word Wizards mobile application ("App").

We are committed to protecting the privacy of all our users, especially children. Word Wizards complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act ("COPPA") and other applicable privacy laws.

Information We Collect

Information Provided During Registration

For Student Accounts (Children):

  • Wizard name (display name chosen by the child)
  • Birth year (to verify age and ensure COPPA compliance)
  • Parent/guardian email address (required for children under 13)

For Parent Accounts:

  • Email address
  • Display name

For Teacher Accounts:

  • Email address
  • Display name
  • School/organization name (optional)

Information Collected Automatically

When using Word Wizards, we automatically collect:

  • Game progress and scores
  • Learning achievements and badges earned
  • Time spent in the app
  • Device type and operating system
  • App performance data

Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect:

  • Real names of children (only wizard names)
  • Physical addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Photos or videos
  • Precise geolocation
  • Social media accounts
  • Any information beyond what is necessary for the App to function

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  1. Provide the Service — Create accounts, save game progress, and deliver educational content
  2. Track Learning Progress — Show parents and teachers how children are progressing
  3. Improve the App — Understand how users interact with Word Wizards to make it better
  4. Communicate — Send important updates about accounts or the service (not marketing)
  5. Ensure Safety — Protect against fraud and maintain a safe environment

Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

We take children's privacy very seriously. Word Wizards is designed for children ages 7–14 and complies with COPPA.

Parental Consent

  • We require a parent or guardian's email address before creating an account for a child under 13
  • Parents receive a verification email and must approve their child's account within 48 hours
  • Without parental verification, the child's account functionality is limited

Parental Rights

Parents and guardians have the right to:

  • Review their child's personal information
  • Request deletion of their child's account and data
  • Refuse further collection of their child's information
  • Access their child's learning progress through the Parent Dashboard

To exercise these rights, contact us at: privacy@literarygenius.academy

Data Minimization

We only collect information that is reasonably necessary to provide the educational service. We do not condition a child's participation on disclosing more information than is necessary.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade any user information to third parties.

We may share limited information with:

  • Supabase — Our database provider that stores account and progress data securely
  • Stripe — Our payment processor for subscription payments (Stripe's privacy policy governs payment data: stripe.com/privacy)

We require all service providers to maintain the confidentiality and security of user data.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect user data, including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Secure database storage with access controls
  • Regular security reviews
  • Limited employee access to user data

Data Retention

We retain user data for as long as the account is active. When an account is deleted:

  • Personal information is removed within 30 days
  • Anonymized usage data may be retained for service improvement
  • Backup copies are purged within 90 days

Your Rights

All users have the right to:

  • Access their personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of their account and data
  • Opt out of non-essential communications

California residents may have additional rights under the CCPA. Contact us for more information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of material changes by posting the updated policy in the App and updating the "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
